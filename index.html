A trial on the Kilian KTP 720X

Metformin doesn't like to be pressed.

A ROMACO-published KTP 720X trial held tablet-weight variation within ±1% at 80 RPM.

Metformin is hygroscopic, flows poorly, and often makes up more than 80% of the tablet. That makes it a quick test of whether a press can hold weight at speed.

Questions engineers, QA and procurement ask

Do all tablet products need granulation and coating?

No. Direct compression works for suitable formulations, and coating depends on the product and market requirements. The route is chosen by formulation, not set in advance.

Can we evaluate just one processing stage?

Each lab and equipment line can be discussed on its own.

Does ROMACO perform IQ/OQ/PQ?

ROMACO provides installation, commissioning and FAT/SAT within the agreed scope. Qualification and validation stay part of your site's quality system.

Will it fit our existing equipment, MES or ERP?

That depends on the model, interfaces and how the project is scoped, and gets reviewed with application and engineering.

What does a lab trial include, how long does it take, and is it paid?

What about warranty, spare parts and service?

ROMACO offers technical support, spare parts, training, retrofits and upgrades.

Is ROMACO a contract manufacturer or packer?

No. ROMACO supplies process equipment and application support. You manufacture your own product.
